Course Overview

This postgraduate core course connects the microscopic details of constituents to macroscopic thermal properties of bulk matter. It covers classical and quantum ensembles, distribution laws, transport equations, fluctuations, and modern models of phase transitions.

Weekly Schedule

Weeks Unit / Module Topics Covered Learning Outcome
Weeks 1–3 Unit I: Fundamentals Entropy, thermodynamic potentials, phase space, Liouville theorem, microcanonical, canonical, and grand canonical ensembles. CO1 CO2
Weeks 4–6 Unit II: Classical Statistics Micro/macro states, Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution, equipartition theorem, partition functions, microcanonical perfect gas. CO3
Weeks 7–9 Unit III: Quantum Statistics Density matrix, Bose-Einstein & Fermi-Dirac distributions, blackbody radiation, BEC, liquid Helium, electron gas. CO3 CO5
Weeks 10–12 Unit IV: Transport & Fluctuations Boltzmann transport equations, thermal conductivity, energy fluctuations, random walk, Brownian movement, Fokker-Planck. CO4
Weeks 13–15 Unit V: Phase Transitions First/second order transitions, critical exponents, Yang-Lee theory, 1D Ising model, Bragg-Williams approximation. CO2 CO6
